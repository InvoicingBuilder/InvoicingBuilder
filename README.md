# InvoicingBuilder – Flexible Invoice Builder with Custom Layout

Most invoice tools look good at first… until you actually try to use them.

As freelancers, we kept running into the same issues:
- Templates were too rigid  
- Couldn’t place signature where we wanted  
- Number/date formats didn’t match my EU clients  
- Could rename fields, but couldn’t change layout  
- Free tools added watermarks, ads, or limits  

So we built **InvoicingBuilder** — a simple invoice builder that gives you real control over how your invoice looks and works.

👉 **Try it here:** https://www.invoicingbuilder.com/

---

## 🚀 What makes this invoice builder different

Instead of locking you into fixed templates, you can:

- Customize invoice layout (logo, signature, notes, etc.)  
- Rename labels and use your own language (useful for EU and international clients)  
- Adjust number and date formats based on region  
- Add taxes (VAT, GST, etc.)  
- Apply discounts (flat or percentage)  
- Include shipping charges  
- Add text or image watermarks
- Bulk invoice generator (Excel/CSV to PDF invoice)
- PDF invoices from tracked hours in **Clockify, Toggl Track, and ClickUp**
- Developer **API**
- PDF invoices from **Notion**

It’s designed to handle real-world billing, not just basic demo invoices.

## For Clockify users

InvoicingBuilder is available as an external add-on in the [Clockify Marketplace](https://marketplace.cake.com/addons/1bdd9fc5-8d1d-4557-979f-8b885cb3b97b). Connect your Clockify account, bring in tracked time, map it to your invoice template, and create an invoice without re-entering your time entries.

## 💡 Why we built this invoice builder

We didn’t set out to build “another invoice generator”.

We just wanted something that:
- Doesn’t get in the way  
- Works for different formats and clients  
- Supports different languages and regional formats  
- Gives control without being complicated  

If you’ve ever felt limited by invoice templates, this might be useful.
